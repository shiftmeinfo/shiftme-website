Booking policy

Payment

You pay the full price of your move online when you book, including any mileage, stairs and extras. Payments are taken securely by Stripe. Your date and your ShiftMe team are confirmed as soon as payment goes through. We don't start any work until payment is received. If you ask for extra work on the day, it must be paid before we start it.

Call outs

Call outs are for small jobs of up to 5 items or 10 boxes and include up to 2 hours with the van and your movers. Extra time is £45 an hour, so please add it when you book if your job might run over. If your booking is bigger than a call out, we'll move it to the right flat or house package and the difference must be paid before we start.

Stairs

Ground and 1st floor moves are included. Where there's no lift, we charge £20 for each floor above the 1st, at each address. Please choose the right floor and access when you book, as this is added to your total automatically.

Parking

We cover paid parking near your addresses, such as pay and display bays and car parks. If your address needs a resident permit or a bay suspension, please arrange it or let us know in your booking notes.

Heavy and specialist items

Pianos, American fridge freezers, safes, gym equipment, hot tubs, marble or stone furniture and any single item over 100kg are quote on request. Please contact us before booking. If one of these items is on the day and wasn't agreed in advance, we may not be able to move it.

Moves outside London

Removals that start or finish outside London have an extra labour charge of 30p per mile for the full round trip, from London to your pickup, on to your new home and back to London. It's worked out from your postcodes and added to your total when you book.

Cancellations

If you cancel 7 or more days before your move, we'll refund your payment in full. If you cancel 3 to 6 days before, we'll refund half. Cancellations with less than 72 hours' notice, or no shows, are non refundable.

Changing your date

Need a different date? You can move your booking once for free with at least 72 hours' notice, subject to availability. Just get in touch and we'll sort it.
