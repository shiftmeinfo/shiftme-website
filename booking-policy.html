Booking policy

Your deposit

Your deposit is 30% of your package price. It secures your date and your ShiftMe team. It is paid when you book, and the remaining balance is due on the day of your move.

Cancellations

If you cancel 7 or more days before your move, we'll refund your deposit in full. If you cancel 3 to 6 days before, we'll refund half. Cancellations with less than 72 hours' notice, or no shows, are non refundable.

Changing your date

Need a different date? You can move your booking once for free with at least 72 hours' notice, subject to availability. Just get in touch and we'll sort it.
